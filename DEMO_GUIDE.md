# App Name TBD — Demo guide

A preview of our Health-a-thon 2026 app (Maternal & Child Health track). It helps a hospital make sure nothing planned for a mother and her baby is missed, from pregnancy through delivery to the baby's vaccines. Everything runs **on the phone with made-up (synthetic) patients**. No real data, and no internet needed.

## Install (Android)
1. Open the APK file on your phone.
2. If asked, allow **"Install unknown apps"** for the app you opened it from (WhatsApp / Files / Drive).
3. Tap **Install**. The app appears as **App Name TBD** with a pink heart icon.

## Things to try
- **Care Team worklist** (Dr. Priya): Now / Today / This week. Every row opens the screen that resolves it: a call-back, a missed visit, a result to review, a referral.
- **A patient** (Patients → Lakshmi K): "Still due", the **Consultation brief** (AI draft you verify), **Record visit**, **Edit tags**, the **Timeline** tab (it splits into mother + baby lanes after delivery).
- **The full loop on one phone:** as Lakshmi, tap **+** → *Ask the hospital to call me* → tick a sign → Send. Sign out, log in as Dr. Priya, and it's at the top of the worklist.
- **Delivery:** Lakshmi → *Admit / record delivery* → save → open the baby → complete the **discharge checklist** → the follow-up plan appears. Then log in as Lakshmi to see **My baby** and the vaccine card.
- **Family app in Kannada / Hindi:** switch language on the welcome screen or in *Me*.
- **Demo tools** (Care Team → avatar → Profile): time travel (+1 / +7 days to see missed visits appear), simulate offline, lock now, and **Reset demo data**.

## Good to know
- Data resets whenever the app is fully closed. That's by design for the demo.
- The app never scores risk or interprets values; clinicians tag risk and the app makes sure the plan is followed through.
- Kannada/Hindi text and health-education content are drafts pending review.
- The AI brief and photo transcription are demo versions that run on the phone.
